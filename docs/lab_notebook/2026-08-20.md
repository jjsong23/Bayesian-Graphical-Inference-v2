## Selective PKA catalytic-subunit knockout node evidence

*Historical note: this section records the initial site-count-adjusted
implementation. It was superseded later the same day by the site-centric
revision documented below; the later counts and output filenames are current.*

Added the supplied `Phosphopeptides-PKAsKO.xlsx` dataset to the configurable
node-selection workflow as two separately selectable evidence streams:

- PKA-Cα knockout phosphoprotein response (`Prkaca` null versus matched intact);
- PKA-Cβ knockout phosphoprotein response (`Prkacb` null versus matched intact).

The streams are disabled in the generic default, preserving the validated
871-protein plus 20-messenger baseline. Both are tagged with dependence group
`pka_subunit_ko_phosphoproteomics` because the comparisons were measured in the
same TMT experiment.

The workbook contains 4,635 mouse mpkCCD phosphopeptide rows. Records were
grouped by UniProt accession and normalized site-pattern label. Median signed
LFC and median Pmod were calculated within each duplicate group, reducing the
table to 3,805 unique site-pattern records. Pmod was retained for audit and
tie-breaking only; it was not used in the Bayes-factor calculation.

Mapping used the 9,170-protein signaling catalog's UniProt accession first and
exact mouse gene-symbol fallback second. A total of 1,680 site patterns mapped
to 776 signaling candidates. The remaining 2,125 source site patterns were
retained in the mapping audit.

Each comparison used its own background of all 3,805 finite,
duplicate-collapsed absolute LFCs before signaling-universe filtering. The q75
values were 0.22603867 for PKA-Cα KO and 0.18371188 for PKA-Cβ KO. Protein
evidence used the maximum absolute LFC and the existing maximum-of-n adjustment
`q_n = 0.75^(1/n)`, followed by the complement-of-minimum likelihood divided by
0.5 to give neutral BF 1.

At default normalization and unit weight:

- PKA-Cα KO positively supported 158 candidates;
- PKA-Cβ KO positively supported 167 candidates;
- 61 candidates were positively supported by both;
- enabling α alone selected 967 proteins;
- enabling β alone selected 992 proteins;
- enabling both selected 1,054 proteins, or 1,074 nodes after the 20 curated
  second messengers were added.

The use of absolute LFC means increases and decreases both support node
participation. Separate streams prevent opposite alpha- and beta-specific signs
from canceling, while signed values remain available for audit. These additions
are protein-level differential-phosphorylation streams; they do not infer new
alpha- or beta-specific KinasePredictor activity profiles.

## Per-evidence-stream preferred Tq controls

Replaced the single global phosphoproteomic preferred-Tq calibration setting
with an independent preferred Tq/reference multiplier for every primary node
and edge evidence stream. The new value is displayed on each evidence card only
as a calibration control. It defines that stream's regularization anchor and is
not applied directly to an ordinary uncalibrated analysis. STRING and STITCH use
the label `Preferred Ref ×`; other streams use `Preferred Tq ×`.

Scientific defaults were preserved. Ordinary datasets prefer 1.0. PKA double-KO
kinase activity, PKA double-KO phosphoprotein response, selective PKA-Cα-KO and
PKA-Cβ-KO phosphoprotein responses, and KinasePredictor prefer 0.1. Ordinary
preferences and fitted multipliers are bounded from 0.25 to 4; the five
phosphoproteomic streams are bounded from 0.05 to 1. Derived scaffold closure
remains fixed because it has no empirical Tq/reference parameter. Older saved
configurations using the former global phosphoproteomic preference are migrated
to the five designated streams unless a stream already supplies its own value.

## Site-centric phosphoprotein node evidence revision

Removed the `q^(1/n)` site-count adjustment from the double-KO, PKA-Cα-KO,
and PKA-Cβ-KO protein-level phosphoprotein streams. Every observed site is now
scored against the stream's one unadjusted all-site q75. A protein inherits the
maximum site-level BF, so at least one site with BF greater than 1 is sufficient
for a positive protein hit. Additional sites neither raise the threshold nor
have their BFs multiplied. Detected-site counts and site-level P/Pmod values
remain in the audit only.

After rebuilding the evidence tables, the double-KO stream supported 465 of
1,157 observed signaling candidates. The selective PKA-Cα and PKA-Cβ streams
supported 267 and 261 of 776 observed candidates, respectively, with 131
supported by both. The current generic defaults select 1,051 proteins (1,071
nodes with 20 messengers); enabling both selective subunit streams selects
1,232 proteins (1,252 total nodes).
